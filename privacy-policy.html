Privacy Policy for Continuum: Consistency Tracker

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Introduction

Continuum: Consistency Tracker ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Continuum does not collect any personal information, and we never see your habit data. Specifically, we do not:

  • Collect your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers
  • Receive your habit names, the days you marked done, or your reminder times
  • Store your habit data on our servers
  • Sell or share anything with third parties, or use it for advertising
  • Track you across other apps or websites
  • Require user accounts or registration

Anonymous Usage (since version 3.8.1)

To learn whether people get through setup and keep coming back, the app sends a few anonymous signals to our own server:

  • A random ID created on your device the first time the app opens. It isn't linked to you, your Apple ID or your device, and deleting the app discards it.
  • One of six event names: first open, updated from an earlier version, finished setup, added a first habit, marked a first day done, and opened the app today (at most once a day).
  • The date and the app version.
  • Since version 3.9, once a day, a few numbers about how the app is used: how many habits you track and how many are archived, how many are strong, your overall consistency, each habit's consistency percentage as an unlabeled list sorted high to low, how many habits you did yesterday, how many have reminders on, and which sort order you picked.

That's the whole list. Habit names, the days you marked and your reminder times are never sent, and the percentages carry no labels, so none can be matched to a habit. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share Anonymous Usage, and nothing more is sent after that.

Feedback You Send

If you write to us through Send Feedback or the one-time check-in, we receive the text you typed, the app version and your iOS version. It isn't connected to the usage ID. If you include your email address so we can reply, we use it only for that.

Where Your Data Lives

Your habit data, including:

  • Habit names
  • The days you marked done
  • Reminder times and settings

is stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you're signed in to iCloud, the app also syncs it through your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit) so your other devices stay up to date. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and we can't read it.

Works Offline

Continuum works without an internet connection. When iCloud is on and you're online, Apple syncs your habits between your devices. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Third-Party Services

We don't use any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. The anonymous usage signals and feedback go to a small server we run on Cloudflare. iCloud sync is provided by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Data Security

We never hold your habits themselves (names, days or reminders), so there is none of it on our side to breach. The anonymous usage signals and feedback are sent over HTTPS. Data synced through iCloud is protected by Apple's iCloud security.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.
