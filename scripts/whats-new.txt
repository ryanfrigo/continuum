Continuum runs on one number now. Your consistency, the share of the last 66 days you showed up, sits on every card in big type with an arrow for how it moved this week. The home screen pools every habit into one figure.

I built this app around streaks for a year, freezes included. That was the wrong number. A streak turns one bad day into zero; consistency turns it into a dip. Freezes are gone. So is the 8pm "streak ends at midnight" alert, replaced by a single nudge on the evening after a miss. Never miss twice.

A habit is formed at 66 days done, in any order. Already past 66? Your next check-in does it.

New habits don't start near zero any more: the number counts from your first day, so 20 days out of 20 reads 100% (it used to read 30%).

The stats screen shows your last 12 weeks as bars. Watch them climb.
