The old icon is back. It's the one with the missing square in the second row, which I swapped out for 3.8 thinking the bottom corner read cleaner, and then missed within a week.

Settings has a Send Feedback row now. Two weeks after your first habit, the app also asks once how it's going. I read every reply. Honestly, I don't know what most of you think of Continuum — that's the gap this closes.

The app now sends a few anonymous signals too: whether setup got finished, and whether you come back. A random ID. Never your habits (the privacy policy lists every signal). Turn it off in Settings → Share Anonymous Usage.
