New colours. A habit used to top out at blue after one perfect month, and then it stopped changing. Now it keeps going: dark red, orange, yellow, green, then blue, purple and pink.

Two things set the colour: the last four weeks, and how long you've kept the habit up. A perfect first month gets you to green. Blue comes around day 66; pink takes about six months. Miss a stretch and it slides back down — at any age.
